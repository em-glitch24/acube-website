About Acube Technologies Inc.

At Acube Technologies Inc., we bridge education and innovation by developing cutting-edge IoT and AI solutions for both learning and industry applications. We are committed to making technology education hands-on, accessible, and future-ready, empowering students, educators, and professionals with practical skills that meet the demands of the modern world.

Beyond our renowned training programs and bootcamps, Acube also delivers AI automation systems and IoT-based industrial solutions, helping organizations integrate smart technologies into their operations. Our growing portfolio combines education, innovation, and real-world impact, positioning us at the forefront of emerging technology transformation in the Philippines.
